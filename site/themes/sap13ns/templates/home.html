{{T "Templates, stylesheets, icons and translations are compiled into the executable. Copy it to a server, point it at PostgreSQL and let nginx talk to it over a Unix socket."}}

  • {{icon "check"}}{{T "Database migrations run on start, inside a transaction."}}
  • {{icon "check"}}{{T "No open port: nginx connects through a socket only its group can use."}}
  • {{icon "check"}}{{T "Updates are atomic: the old binary or the new one, never a mix."}}
  • {{icon "check"}}{{T "Node.js is only used at build time. Production never sees it."}}
