## Visiting the website

### No tracking cookies

We do not use advertising, tracking or social media cookies, and we do not load third-party scripts, fonts or embeds. That is why there is no cookie banner.

The only cookies are technical and strictly necessary:

- a **session cookie**, only if you sign in, to keep you signed in;
- a short-lived **notice cookie** that carries a one-line message (such as "Saved") from one page to the next after an action in the admin panel, deleted after it is shown.

Your choice of light or dark mode is stored in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us.

### Cookie-less analytics

To know which pages are read, we count visits in aggregate: pages viewed, referring sites and similar totals. This is done without cookies and without building a profile of you. The statistics we keep contain no personal data.

### Server logs

Like any web server, ours records technical information about requests (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser) to keep the service secure and to investigate abuse. These logs are kept for a short period and then deleted.

### Comments

If comments are enabled on a post and you write one, we store the name, e-mail address and optional website you enter, the text, and your IP address and browser for spam control. Your e-mail address is never published.

## Using our hosting

### Account and billing

When you create an account we handle your name, e-mail address, password (stored only as an Argon2id hash) and, for commercial plans, billing details. We use them to provide the service, to bill you and to contact you about your account.

**Payments are processed by Stripe** (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland). Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your card number. Stripe acts under its own privacy policy, and may transfer data outside the European Economic Area with appropriate safeguards.

### Sites you publish

For the content and the visitors of a site you host with us, you are the data controller and we process that data on your behalf, only to provide the hosting. If your site collects personal data, you are responsible for informing your visitors.

## Legal bases

- **Contract:** to provide the hosting you signed up for and to manage your account.
- **Legal obligation:** to keep invoices and accounting records.
- **Legitimate interest:** to keep the website and the service secure, prevent abuse and produce aggregate statistics without personal data.
- **Consent:** where we ask for it, for example to publish a comment. You can withdraw it at any time.

## How long we keep data

- Account data: while your account exists, and up to 30 days after you close it, unless you ask for earlier deletion.
- Invoices and billing records: for the periods required by tax and commercial law.
- Server logs: a short period, then deleted.
- Comments: while the comment is published, or until you ask us to remove it.

## Who else receives data

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. We only share it with providers that help us run the service, such as Stripe for payments and our hosting and e-mail providers, under data processing agreements, and with authorities when the law requires it.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **rectify** inaccurate data;
- **erase** your data;
- **restrict** how we process it;
- **data portability**, receiving your data in a common machine-readable format;
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interest;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Write to victor@bauber.xyz to exercise any of them. We will reply within one month. If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can also complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the **Agencia Española de Protección de Datos** ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)).
