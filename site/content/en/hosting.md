You can run sap13ns on your own server; it is open source and always will be. If you would rather not think about servers, we host it for you on sap13ns.site, on the same software we publish.

Current prices are shown on this page and at signup.

## Two kinds of site

### Personal sites — free

For a blog, a portfolio, a CV, notes from a hobby or a family site. Personal sites are free, with one condition: they must be **non-commercial**. That means no shop, no paid services, no advertising and no promotion of a business.

Every new personal site is **checked by a person** before it goes public. We look at what the site is for, not at what you write about. Usually this is quick; if we have questions, we write to you. We may refuse a site, or ask you to move to a commercial plan, when it is clearly commercial.

### Commercial sites — paid

For companies, freelancers, shops, newsletters with sponsors, client projects and anything else that makes money or promotes a business. Commercial plans are billed monthly through **Stripe**, and you can cancel at any time.

## Included in every plan

- HTTPS certificates, issued and renewed automatically.
- Updates to new sap13ns versions, applied by us.
- Regular backups of your content and uploads.
- All six themes, with light and dark modes.
- The Markdown editor, translations, feeds, comments and the WordPress importer.
- Cookie-less statistics, with no consent banner needed.
- One account for all your sites.

Commercial plans add your **own domain** and room for your **own themes and plugins**. Plugins you upload are reviewed by us before they can be switched on, and then pinned to the exact file we reviewed.

## Leaving is easy

Your content is yours. You can export it at any time and move it to your own sap13ns server, to another host, or anywhere else. Since sap13ns is open source, the export works with the same software you would run yourself.

## FAQ

### What counts as commercial?

A site that sells something, advertises, collects payments or exists to promote a company or a professional service. A personal blog where you sometimes mention your job is fine. A portfolio that is your business's website is commercial. When in doubt, ask us before you sign up.

### Why do free sites need validation?

Free hosting attracts spam, phishing and copied content. A short human check keeps the platform clean for everyone and protects the reputation of every site's address.

### Can I start free and upgrade later?

Yes. You can move a personal site to a commercial plan at any time, and keep its content, settings and address.

### How do payments work?

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. You get an invoice for every charge.

### What happens when I cancel?

Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that the site goes offline, and you have time to export your content before it is deleted.

### Can I use my own theme or plugin?

On commercial plans, yes. Themes are checked automatically when you upload them: no scripts, no external forms, bounded rendering, and the same strict security rules as the built-in ones. Plugins run in the WebAssembly sandbox and are reviewed by us before they can be enabled.

### Who is responsible for my data?

BAUBER.XYZ SL, a company based in Barcelona, Spain. Your data is handled under the GDPR, and the [privacy policy](/privacy) explains what we keep, why and for how long.

### Do you show ads on my site?

No. We do not add advertising, trackers or third-party scripts to hosted sites. The only addition is the small "Powered by sap13ns" credit in the footer.
